# Timer music license

`calm.mp3`, `focus.mp3`, `upbeat.mp3` and `tick.mp3` are original works made for Veevee. They are synthesized
from plain sine tones by `scripts/dev/make-timer-music.sh`; no recording, sample or third-party music is used.

They are released under CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication,
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). They are royalty-free for any use, with no attribution needed.

The chime at zero is generated in the browser by `src/lib/canvas/chime.js` and is not a file.
